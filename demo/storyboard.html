Why it breaks
Line totals are now memoised per SKU on first use. The test adds 3 mugs at 39.60 (118.80), then the catalog price rises to 40.00 and Cart.reprice() updates the unit price — but the cached 118.80 is returned instead of 120.00. PR #412 requires totals to reflect current catalog prices and PR #431 describes the cache as a pure performance change, so the commit is the defect, not the test.
